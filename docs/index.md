# Workshop recording: QGIS Essentials

This page provides a video recording of the Map and Data Library's introductory QGIS workshop. 

### Workshop description

Are you interested in exploring the world of Geographic Information Systems (GIS)? Join us for a free, hands-on workshop designed for new learners and those looking to refresh their skills.

This session from U of T’s 
<a href="https://mdl.library.utoronto.ca/" target="_blank">Map and Data Library</a>
will introduce you to **QGIS3**, a robust, cross-platform, open source desktop GIS software, and guide you through the essentials of working with spatial data.

### What to expect

- **Creating Projects**: Organize your work, combining data sources, maps, and tools.
- **Adding Data**: Work with a variety of spatial and non-spatial datasets from various sources.
- **Exploring and Managing Data**: Use attribute tables, understand map projections, and perform geoprocessing.
- **Symbology and Labeling**: Design data-driven cartographic visualizations.
- **Resources**: How you can access ongoing support from the Map and Data Library, including help finding data and one-on-one consultations.

Along the way, we’ll discuss GIS fundamentals and workflows to help you understand how spatial data can support your research. Don’t miss this opportunity to build valuable technical skills and take your first steps with QGIS. We'll look forward to seeing you there!

This is a beginner-friendly workshop, with no recommended prerequisites. **You will need, however, to have the QGIS3 software installed on your computer to follow along with the technical segment. Download it for free here: <a href="https://qgis.org/download/" target="_blank">https://qgis.org/download</a>.**

**You'll also need the workshop data, which can be downloaded here: 
<a href="https://maps.library.utoronto.ca/workshops/QGIS_Essentials/QGISEssentials.zip">https://maps.library.utoronto.ca/workshops/QGIS_Essentials/QGISEssentials.zip</a>.** 

**Instructor**: <a href="https://library.utoronto.ca/staff/cole-white" target="_blank">Cole White</a>, GIS Analyst, Map and Data Library
